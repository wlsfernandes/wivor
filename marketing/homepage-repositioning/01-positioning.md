# Positioning and message hierarchy

## The promise

**For athletes:** Find the photos from your event and choose the moments you want to keep.

**For photographers:** Publish event work where participating athletes can find it and buy it.

**For organizers:** Give participants a path to professional event photos through WiVor's photographer network. Keep this message subordinate on the homepage.

WiVor is a sports-event photo marketplace. The brand should feel athletic and human because of real race photography, and reliable because the interface and claims are precise.

## Audience order on the homepage

1. **Athletes** arrive to find photos. Give them the headline, search, real gallery proof, and purchase reassurance.
2. **Photographers** need to understand the opportunity, workflow, commission model, and application path. Give them a distinct section with a clear CTA.
3. **Organizers** need a concise partnership path lower on the page.

The homepage should direct each audience to an existing working journey. Do not make each audience equally prominent in the first viewport.

## Message ladder

1. **Category:** Race and sports-event photos.
2. **Outcome:** Find your own moments and keep the ones you love.
3. **Method:** Search for an event, then use bib search or optional selfie search when enabled, or browse the gallery.
4. **Proof:** Real events and real available galleries, only when backed by current data and permitted imagery.
5. **Trust:** Preview before purchase; buy only the selected photos; explain privacy accurately.

Lead with the outcome and action. Explain mechanics after the visitor knows why they matter.

## Voice

- Short, direct, warm, and specific.
- Speak to the visitor: “Find your race photos,” “Search your event,” “Preview before you buy.”
- Prefer observable benefits over platform language such as “innovative marketplace” or “advanced technology.”
- Avoid urgency, guaranteed results, fabricated scale, unsupported earnings claims, and claims about “instant” discovery.
- Use **WiVor** in public copy, following the frontend specification.

## Visual principle

Keep the approved Manrope/Inter typography, charcoal and off-white surfaces, and `#FF3D00` orange accent. Use orange to identify the primary action and a few deliberate highlights. Use authentic, authorized race photos for energy. Do not create an orange-heavy page or synthetic event imagery.

## Claims to verify before publishing

- Whether selfie search is enabled on the deployed environment.
- Exact photographer commission and fee language.
- Whether a gallery is live and whether its images may appear on the homepage.
- Any stated event or photo counts, pricing, discounts, and delivery timing.
- Photographer workflow benefits such as earnings reporting, if mentioned.

If a claim cannot be verified, omit it or use narrower wording.
